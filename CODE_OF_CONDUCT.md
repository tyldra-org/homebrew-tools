# Code of conduct

> How do people treat each other here?

This tap is a small project with one maintainer. These rules apply in
issues, pull requests, reviews and anywhere else you represent the project.

## Expected

- Be direct and kind. Criticize the work, not the person.
- Assume good intent, and ask before you conclude.
- Back claims with evidence, and say when you are unsure.
- Keep reports focused and reproducible; other people's time matters.

## Not accepted

- Harassment, insults, threats or sexual language, in public or in private.
- Attacks on anyone's identity, background or experience.
- Publishing someone's private information without their permission.
- Continuing after you have been asked to stop.

## Reporting

Email yogeshprasad098@gmail.com. Only the maintainer reads reports, and they
stay private. Depending on what happened, the maintainer may edit or remove
contributions or block someone from the project, for a time or for good.
